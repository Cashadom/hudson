HUDSON NAPROOM / PASSENGER COMFORT

A private pause.
A better journey.

Personal space, between destinations.

Hudson commercializes premium enclosed rest cabins designed to give travelers a quiet, individual space for relaxation during a connection, a delay or a long journey.

Developed with specialized OEM manufacturing partners, Naproom combines compact cabin architecture, adjustable lighting, controlled access and airflow with configurable interior comfort. Each installation is planned around airport operations and passenger needs.

Product concept based on the GY802-A and GY803-A supplier series. Final configuration, approvals and availability are subject to technical and commercial validation.

HUDSON NAPROOM / PRODUCT PRESENTATION / naproom.png
CABIN FEATURES / SUPPLIER REFERENCE

Designed for a considered rest.

Compact, configurable and designed for indoor, air-conditioned settings. Hudson works with manufacturing partners on fit-out, branding, installation guidance and operating requirements.

01 / FORM FACTOR

Compact footprint

Approx. 2.30 × 1.148 × 1.747 m external dimensions; approx. 2.64 m² cabin footprint, excluding aisles and servicing space.

02 / ACCESS

Private, enclosed space

Sliding-door design with card access and internal safety latch. PIN options may be available. Booking and payment integration require evaluation.

03 / COMFORT

Personal lighting controls

Adjustable lighting, reading light, charging connections and an interior console. Reclining seating or mattresses are separately configured.

04 / AIRFLOW

Adjustable circulation

Supplier reference of approximately 30 m³/h airflow. The standard cabin does not include independent air conditioning.

05 / ELECTRICAL

Low-voltage interior

110–240 V input with a 12 V internal supply as quoted by the supplier. Site electrical compliance must be assessed locally.

06 / OPERATIONS

Airport-ready planning

OEM branding, finishes and layout options. Washable surfaces, disposable protective covers and hygiene workflows are customization requirements under evaluation.

Specifications refer to the supplier's preliminary GY802-A / GY803-A proposal of 12 October 2026. Fire-retardant material options, smoke detection, washability modifications, installation and regulatory suitability require confirmation. Supplier quotes a two-year product warranty; final terms are subject to contract. No airport certification or live software integration is claimed here.
