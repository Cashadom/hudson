TECHNICAL CONSIDERATIONS

Details that
matter.

Cabin installation requires more than a footprint. The final configuration must be assessed for its intended use, the premises and applicable regulations.

Information below describes the planned approach, not an approval or certification of either product.

Installation & spatial requirements

Both cabins are supplied as modular assemblies. The site must provide a level, load-bearing floor, sufficient delivery access, appropriate door clearance and service space. Electrical connection and any local structural or operational constraints must be reviewed before quotation.

Power & ventilation

The Compact platform uses a standard-compatible 230 V supply and specifies adjustable ventilation of approximately 30 m³/h. The Confort platform supports 100–240 V and specifies a fan with a claimed maximum capacity of 320 m³/h. Actual occupied-cabin air quality and airflow must be checked for the final configuration and host building.

Cleaning & operational use

Both formats are intended for one occupant at a time. Washable surfaces and carpet-free flooring are proposed to facilitate routine cleaning. Between-session cleaning and re-opening procedures must be established with the operator; no automated cleaning status is assumed to be available today.

Door access & emergency release

Access-control options, including temporary PIN credentials, are under technical evaluation. Compatibility with third-party software is not confirmed. A safe means of exit from inside and appropriate emergency access must be maintained independently of a cloud booking service.

Fire safety & regulatory requirements

Supplier component reports do not establish compliance of the complete installed cabin. Fire reaction classifications, electrical safety, accessibility, evacuation and any premises-specific obligations must be reviewed before supply or installation. No universal CE or public-premises approval is claimed.
